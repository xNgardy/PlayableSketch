What makes a good sketch?

Draw rooms from above, including rooms with bends. Leave a gap with a small tick at each end for a door. Numbers are optional. One floor at a time.

How it works

  1. 01

    Upload a drawing

    Choose a photo or scan of your floor plan. Mark doors with a gap and a small tick at each end.

  2. 02

    Review your plan

    Check the suggested rooms and doors. Adjust the shapes, connections and sizes.

  3. 03

    Build your level

    Confirm your plan, then export it for Unreal. Choose a start and goal for a playable level.

No measurements needed. Suggested sizes can be changed later.
