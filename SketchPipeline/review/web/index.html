Help and colors

Wall / Opening: select two points. Drawing an opening on a wall cuts that wall across the selected span.

Join: select two wall endpoints. Scroll to zoom. Hold Space or use Pan to move around.

Teal: walls. Orange: pending opening. Green: approved opening. Amber: uncertain region. Red: unsupported region or dangling wall.

Delete removes the selected element. Escape cancels an operation. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes a change.
