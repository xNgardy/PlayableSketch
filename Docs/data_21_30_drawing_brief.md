# Data_21–Data_30: next tablet drawing brief

These are requested future drawings, not annotations. Actual uploaded content always takes precedence over the requested values below.

## Shared rules

- One plan per image: `Data/Data_21.png` through `Data/Data_30.png`. Export original tablet PNGs with opaque light backgrounds and dark handwritten strokes; omit app controls.
- Width × depth means horizontal × vertical. Single wall centerlines, one shared wall, rectangular rooms/corridors. Proportions may be approximate.
- Every written value includes `cm`. Place text parallel to its measured span where practical. For this batch, try vertical handwriting for some vertical dimensions; retain your natural writing style.
- Door location dimensions describe the visible solid wall segment to the near opening edge. A top-corner reference ends at the door's upper edge; a right-corner reference ends at its right edge. Never use the door center as the paper reference.
- Between two doors without an intervening room boundary, label the edge-to-edge solid wall separation. At a T junction/new room boundary, prefer the local junction-to-opening span.
- Corridor width is between boundary wall centerlines. A corner-to-corridor-junction label is a wall centerline span, not a clear entrance-edge offset. Open entrances have no door endpoint marks or `D` labels.
- Door clear widths remain distinct from corridor centerline widths. Wall thickness/height are settings, not paper labels. Do not add Start/Goal or room-name text for this batch.
- All dimensions described below should be written, except values explicitly identified as checks/derived positions or intentionally missing. Repeated shared depths may be written once if scope is clear.
